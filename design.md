### 1. Water Delivery & Steam Routing

* **Steam Transfer Pipe:** Connect a heat-resistant pipe or flexible braided stainless steel hose from the 1/2-inch ball valve on the 20L boiler to the lower entry fitting (the 1-inch collar/nipple) on the 55-gallon main drum.


* **Steam Spreader / Diffuser (Inside the Drum):** Inside the main drum at the bottom, route a perforated pipe (usually a T-junction or cross made of metal pipe with holes drilled along the sides/bottom) so steam disperses evenly across the base rather than blasting in one concentrated spot.
* **False Bottom / False Floor:** Place a sturdy metal grate or perforated stainless steel rack 4 to 6 inches above the bottom of the drum. Substrate bags sit on this platform so they are heated purely by rising atmospheric steam and do not sit in pooling condensation.

### 2. Condensate & Pressure Management

* **Condensate Drain:** The lowest drain valve on the main drum is left cracked open slightly during the run to drain excess condensed water so it doesn't build up and waterlog bottom bags.


* **Top Venting (Crucial for Atmospheric Operation):** An atmospheric sterilizer must **never** build pressure. The lid is either clamped loosely or fitted with an open vent pipe/hole at the very top. Steam must freely push all cold air out of the drum until a steady plume of steam escapes from the top vent.
* **Temperature Monitoring:** A dial thermometer or probe is inserted near the top/middle exhaust to confirm internal chamber temperatures reach 98°C–100°C (208°F–212°F) before starting the sterilization timer (typically running 16 to 24 hours).



### 3. Electrical & Safety Integration for the 2 kW Element

* **Dry-Run Protection / Water Level Float:** A 20-liter boiler running a 2 kW element for up to 24 hours can boil dry. The boiler either needs a continuous float valve feed (gravity or low-pressure water line) or a manual refill/sight-glass tube to monitor water level.


* **Wiring & Relay:** The 2 kW element draws roughly 8.5 to 9 amps at 230V. It should be wired through a heavy-duty industrial contactor or solid-state relay (SSR) controlled by a digital temperature controller (like an STC-1000 or PID/timer), protected with a dedicated circuit breaker (MCB) and proper grounding/earthing.
* **Drum Insulation:** Wrapping the exterior of the 55-gallon steel drum in mineral wool or ceramic fiber insulation with outer strapping cuts heat loss, stabilizes temperatures, and saves power.
