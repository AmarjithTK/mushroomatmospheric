Here is your **Calicut Shopping Master Guide**, formatted specifically for your trip tomorrow. 

The shopping list is organized by the **three commercial market streets in Calicut** so you can walk from store to store in order. All online Amazon items are included with offline equivalents, and **all tiny fasteners, washers, gaskets, and sundries are isolated in a dedicated section at the very end**.

---

## Part 1: Calicut Market Map (Where to Walk)

* **Stop 1: Cherooty Road / Mavoor Road Junction:** The stainless steel piping, dairy equipment, and industrial valve capital. (All SS fittings, float valves, hoses, and camlocks).
* **Stop 2: SM Street / Palayam Market (Behind Police Station):** The wholesale electrical switchgear hub. (Breakers, contactors, relays, sockets, and heavy copper wire).
* **Stop 3: Big Bazar / Palayam Kitchenware Lane:** Commercial hotel kitchenware and cooker repair counters. (Commercial 6 mm cooker vents and whistles).
