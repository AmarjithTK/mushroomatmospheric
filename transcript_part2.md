## Hardware, Materials & Tools Identified in Part 2
* **Boiler Canister:** 20 Liters Stainless Steel Canister (labeled "Rudra", "SHINER").
* **Steam Exit Port:** 1/2-inch stainless steel extension nipple + 1/2-inch brass/SS ball valve + 1/2-inch to 3/4-inch step-up lock connector inside.
* **Heating Element:** IndoSurgical 2 kW 230V immersion heating element (Amazon).
* **Seals & Gaskets:** Rubber washer (inside), red vulcanized fiber washer (outside), automotive red RTV silicone gasket maker.
* **Tools:** Corded power drill with golden step cone drill bit, angle grinder with metal cutting/grinding disc, adjustable pipe wrench / multi-grip pliers.
* **Safety Equipment:** Brown leather work apron, clear safety goggles.
